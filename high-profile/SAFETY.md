# Research scope and safety

HIGH-PROFILE establishes what publicly available evidence supports. It is not a targeting or operational planning system.

The schema stores broad location text and country/region context. It has no military coordinates, aim points, vulnerability rankings, targeting scores, weapon-effect models, attack routes, live movement feeds, patrol tracks or personnel tracking. The application does not infer nuclear armament from platform capability. It does not supply construction or sabotage instructions.

Atlas is deferred until the evidence architecture is mature. Any future geographic interface must remain country/regional or authoritative civilian research geography. It must not introduce precise sensitive military locations or operational overlays.

Imported documents and metadata are untrusted. React escapes rendered content; no raw document HTML is rendered. Database parameters are bound, not concatenated. Local object keys are validated hashes. Current ingestion does not fetch arbitrary URLs and does not execute model instructions. Publication requires reviewed evidence and database-enforced provenance. Production private research and write routes require authentication through a deployment gateway and server-side token.

Reviewers remain responsible for the substantive content of their claims. General-purpose text storage is not a safety classifier. Any future extraction or acquisition service must incorporate editorial scope review before publication and must not convert sensitive operational material into product capabilities.
